# Uber
### Uber Ride Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

**Project Overview**
An interactive **Uber Ride Analysis Dashboard** developed in Power BI to analyze booking activity, revenue performance, customer behavior, trip patterns, and operational efficiency. The dashboard transforms raw Uber trip data into actionable business insights through interactive KPIs and visual analytics.

**Key Analysis Areas**

* **Total Bookings & Revenue:** Monitoring overall booking volume and financial performance.
* **Ride & Distance Analysis:** Analyzing trip distance, ride frequency, and travel patterns.
* **Time-Based Analysis:** Identifying peak hours, busy days, and seasonal booking trends.
* **Vehicle/Service Performance:** Comparing different Uber service or vehicle categories.
* **Location Analysis:** Evaluating pickup and drop-off demand by location.
* **Payment Analysis:** Understanding customer payment preferences.
* **Cancellation Analysis:** Identifying cancellation patterns and their operational impact.
* **Customer Ratings:** Analyzing customer satisfaction and service quality.

**Key KPIs**

* Total Bookings
* Total Revenue
* Average Booking Value
* Total Trip Distance
* Average Trip Distance
* Completed Trips
* Cancelled Trips
* Cancellation Rate
* Average Customer Rating

**Tools & Technologies**

* **Microsoft Power BI**
* **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** — Calculated measures and KPIs
* **Data Modeling** — Relationships and analytical structure
* **Interactive Data Visualization** — Charts, cards, slicers, and filters

**Business Value**
The dashboard helps identify **demand patterns, high-performing services, revenue opportunities, cancellation issues, and customer behavior**, supporting data-driven decisions for improving operational efficiency and customer experience.
